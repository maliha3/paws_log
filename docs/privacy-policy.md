# Privacy Policy for Paws Log

**Last updated: 25 October 2026**

## The short version

Paws Log keeps everything about your cats on your phone. There is no account
to create, no server holding your records, and nothing is collected about how
you use the app.

Nothing you enter ever leaves your device. Not to us, not to anyone.

## What stays on your phone

All of it:

- your cats, their photos, birthdays, breed, microchip and vet details
- reminders and when they are due
- medicines, doses and whether each one was given
- prescriptions, their photographs, and the text read off them
- vet visits, notes, costs and attachments
- weights, vaccinations, conditions and allergies

These live in a database and a folder inside the app's own storage on your
device. They are not uploaded. Nobody else can read them, including the
developer.

Prescriptions are read **on your phone**, using Apple's Vision framework. The
photograph is never sent anywhere to be processed.

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. No server is involved in
deciding when to notify you.

## What leaves your phone

**When you buy or restore the unlock.** Payment is handled entirely by Apple.
Your device talks to Apple's App Store and to nobody else — there is no
service in between, and the developer never sees your card details, your
name or your Apple Account. Apple tells your device whether the purchase
exists; that is the whole exchange.

**When you choose to share something.** Exporting a backup, sharing a photo,
or sending the vet summary hands a file to the iOS share sheet, and you
choose where it goes — Mail, Files, AirDrop, or anywhere else. Once it leaves
that way, it is governed by wherever you sent it. Paws Log does not send any
of it on its own.

**When you tap your vet's phone number**, iOS opens the Phone app. Paws Log
does not place the call or record that you made one.

## What is not collected

- No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No accounts, no sign-in, no email address
- No location
- No contacts
- Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers

## Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To photograph your cat, or scan a prescription. Images are saved on your device. |
| **Photos** | To choose a picture you have already taken. |
| **Notifications** | To tell you when care is due. Scheduled locally; no push server. |

Each is optional. Declining one disables the feature that needs it and
nothing else.

## Children

Paws Log is not directed at children and collects no personal information
from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a pet in the app removes their records and their photo files.
Deleting the app removes everything it ever stored. Neither needs a request
to the developer, because there is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

A purchase is tied to your Apple Account rather than to the app, so it can be
restored after reinstalling.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes
will be noted in the app's release notes.
